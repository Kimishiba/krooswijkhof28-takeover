## ⚡ 1. Sonoff Basic RFR2 Smart Lighting System (WiFi + 433MHz RF)

The lighting system in the apartment uses **Sonoff Basic RFR2** in-line smart modules ([Official Sonoff NL product page](https://www.sonoff.nl/a-57623649/wifi-modules/sonoff-basic-rfr2-wifi-433mhz/)). 
These dual-connectivity modules support both **WiFi** (app control via eWeLink, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, smart schedules) and **433MHz RF** (wireless wall switch panels), enabling full smart-home automation and traditional wall switch operation with zero wall cutting or rewiring.

The complete system is offered as a single package at original module cost price (€15.00/module), with the wall switch panels included for free.

- **Modules (6 units installed)**: In-line switch modules controlling Hallway lights (2), Kitchen, Kitchen Island, Dining Table, and TV accent lights.

- **Smart Features**: eWeLink app (iOS/Android), Apple/Google/Alexa voice control, sunrise/sunset scheduling, timer automation, and remote on/off anywhere in the world.
- **Retail Value**: €18.95/module on sonoff.nl × 6 = €113.70 + switches (~€25) ≈ **€140.00**
